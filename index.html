About

Electronics design meets robot autonomy.

I’m an Assistant Manager at AITEC NCP in Islamabad, where I lead the electronics design on autonomous platforms: custom power boards in Altium, sensor and compute integration on NVIDIA Jetson, and ROS 2 navigation stacks. Before that I worked as a Junior Design Engineer and Junior Test Engineer at BCube.

My current research compares obstacle-avoidance approaches for a drone built on an X500 V2. I’m applying to robotics and AI graduate programs (Erasmus Mundus and professor-led scholarships) starting September 2027.
